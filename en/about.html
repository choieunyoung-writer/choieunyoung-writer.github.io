Choi Eunyoung is a novelist born in 1984 in Gwangmyeong, Gyeonggi Province. She studied Korean Language and Literature at Korea University, and made her debut in 2013 when her novella “Shoko’s Smile” won the Writer’s World (Jakga Segye) New Writer’s Award.

Her books include the story collections Shoko’s Smile (2016), A Person Harmless to Me (2018) and Even with the Faintest Light (2023), the novel Bright Night (2021), the short fiction Effortless (2022), and the essay collection Before the Blank Page (2026). [English titles pending confirmation]
